Slide 1 of 4 • Problem Statement

Campus Coordination Is Fragmented

University student organizations struggle with chaotic, manual workflows that hurt student engagement and administrative compliance.

📋 Disconnected Spreadsheets

Presidents manage event details, volunteer lists, task assignments, and budget spreadsheets across scattered chat groups and shared drives.

📢 Lost Student Opportunities

Students regularly miss announcements or face clumsy registration links with zero real-time seat tracking and no visibility into volunteer tasks.

⏳ Blind Administration

University admin lacks oversight of campus activities, handling financial approvals and event compliance through delayed email threads.

Slide 2 of 4 • Solution Architecture

UniSphere: One Unified Platform, 3 Connected Roles

An end-to-end digital lifecycle connecting Presidents, Students, and Administrators seamlessly.

01
Secure Onboarding Signup & 2-step email OTP with auto-preserved destinations.
02
Society & Proposals President creates society & drafts events with strict PKT timezone validation.
03
Admin Governance Combined event & budget review with approval remarks.
04
Execution & Control Atomic registration, Kanban task tracking, & hard-capped expense ledger.
Slide 3 of 4 • Tech & Engineering Highlights

Engineered for Robustness & Zero Race Conditions

Built on modern Next.js 14 App Router with SQLite and Prisma for maximum speed and simplicity.

⚡ Atomic Concurrency

Direct SQL atomic counter updates (WHERE regCount < capacity) prevent overselling under high simultaneous student load.

🔒 Strict Governance

Pure-function state machines enforce event lifecycle transitions, hard expense caps, and role-based path protection.

📡 Real-Time SSE Stream

Server-Sent Events instantly update public seat counts and deliver in-app notification toasts without manual page refresh.
